Our heritage

A Short History

1835

Msida became a vice-parish, at a time when it was still a small community closely linked to fishing.

1867

Msida was officially established as a parish. Its first parish church was the Church of the Immaculate Conception, also known as the Small Church, in Valley Road.

1886

On 4 April, the foundation stone of the present parish church on the Msida waterfront was laid.

1889

The new church, dedicated to the Patronage of St Joseph, was opened for worship. Msida Parish was the first parish in the Maltese Islands dedicated under this title of St Joseph.

1881–1969

Other parishes were formed from the territory of Msida Parish: Ħamrun in 1881, Santa Venera in 1918, Pietà in 1968 and Ta’ Xbiex in 1969.

2017

The parish celebrated the 150th anniversary of its foundation with various religious activities connected with St Joseph.

2019

By decree dated 19 March 2019, St Joseph Parish Church was granted the title of Archpresbyteral Church.
